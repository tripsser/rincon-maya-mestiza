# Landing NORIX

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: duenos y administradores de restaurantes o marcas que necesitan entender NORIX como sistema operativo restaurantero, ERP/CRM y portal multi-contexto.

Job: entender en segundos que NORIX organiza administracion y operacion en una sola jerarquia: tenant, marca y sucursal.

Primary action: entrar al portal.

Chosen direction: Norvin-inspired commercial premium landing translated into NORIX deep-blue command-center identity. Use modular dark sections, product preview, services, context mechanism, operational flow and final CTA.

Memorable moment: first viewport shows the product mechanism, not a generic hero: context chain and synthetic portal preview demonstrate "one portal, many contexts".

Constraints: no white-dominant identity, no fake testimonials or commercial metrics, no literal copying of Norvin/QuantumFlux/MotionSite.
